Books About Cats

Explore cats in fiction, poetry, and classic stories.

Cats have inspired writers to tell stories full of humor, imagination, and adventure . Here are three works that put cats at the center of the story.

I Am a Cat

In this novel by Natsume Sōseki, a nameless cat observes the people around him. His sharp and humorous commentary offers a different perspective on human society.

Old Possum’s Book of Practical Cats

T. S. Eliot’s poetry collection introduces cats with memorable names and distinct personalities. Its playful verses inspired the musical Cats.

The Cat in the Hat

In this children’s book by Dr. Seuss, a cat wearing a tall striped hat turns a rainy day into a wild adventure. Rhyming language brings the cat’s mischief to life.
