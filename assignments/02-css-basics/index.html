My Favorite Cats

Cats make everyday life a little more interesting.

I like cats because they can be playful, curious, and calm. Here are three things I enjoy about them.

Playful Cats

It is fun to watch a cat chase a toy or jump into an empty cardboard box.

Curious Cats

I enjoy watching cats explore a new room and investigate the world around them.

Sleepy Cats

A cat curled up in a sunny spot always looks comfortable and relaxed.
